## Full description

```
Anytime Workforce is Anytime Diesel’s official employee app for day-to-day workforce operations.

What you can do
• Check in and check out with precise location against your branch
• Optional face verification when your company enables Face Security
• Apply for leave, track balances, and follow approvals
• Work Planner for tasks and day-to-day delivery
• Expenses, HR documents, assets, announcements, and notifications

Who this app is for
Accounts are created by HR or Developer Admin. There is no public self-registration. Use the work email issued by your company.

Privacy & access
• Privacy policy: https://hrms.anytime-diesel.com/privacy
• Terms: https://hrms.anytime-diesel.com/terms
• Account deletion requests: https://hrms.anytime-diesel.com/account-deletion

Permissions
• Location (while using the app): attendance geofencing — not used in the background
• Camera: face registration and check-in only when Face Security is enabled
• Notifications: optional leave, task, and company alerts

Anytime Workforce is operated by Anytime Diesel for internal employees and authorized contractors.
```
